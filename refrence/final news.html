GENRANQ Crosses 120 Engineers and Opens a New Delivery Centre in Vadodara

Vadodara, India — September 24, 2026 — GENRANQ Software LLP, a software and search growth company serving 600+ clients in 42 countries, today announced that it has grown to more than 120 team members and will open a new delivery centre in Vadodara, Gujarat.

The expansion doubles the company's capacity for dedicated developer teams and AI search programs, and creates 40 new roles across engineering, SEO, and design.

Why GENRANQ is expanding

Over the past 18 months, demand has grown sharply from startups and mid-sized businesses that want to build products and grow search visibility with one partner, rather than managing separate agencies for development and marketing.

At the same time, the rise of AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity has created new demand for GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) — an area where GENRANQ has worked since 2024.

"

Our clients don't want five vendors. They want one team that can build the product, ship the website, and make sure customers find it — on Google and inside AI answers. This centre lets us do that at twice the scale.

R
Rohan PatelFounder & CEO, GENRANQ Software LLP

What the new centre includes

New delivery centre, Vadodara

The new 18,000 sq. ft. delivery centre opens on October 15, 2026.

  • Dedicated team pods: private workspaces for long-term client teams, aligned to US, UK, EU, and Middle East time zones.
  • AI Search lab: where the team tracks 20,000+ AI prompts every month across major AI assistants.
  • Training academy: a structured program for new engineers covering code quality, security, and SEO-ready development.
  • Client collaboration rooms: spaces for workshops, sprint reviews, and client visits.
